Four original instrumental compositions created for this portfolio. No external songs, recordings or third-party samples. Source: scripts/prepare-stage-music.py.
Silk Road: minor/modal plucked strings, dulcimer-like melody and restrained low drum.
Opera Garden: pentatonic bowed melody, dulcimer-like accompaniment and light wood percussion.
Rural Fireflies: bright pentatonic flute-like melody and pastoral guitar arpeggios.
White Snake: slower pentatonic flute-like melody and spacious zither-like plucks.
Timbres are synthesized interpretations, not recordings of traditional instruments.
